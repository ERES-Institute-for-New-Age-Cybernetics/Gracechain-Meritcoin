### Worked example: arithmetic only, not a finding

Every number below is invented to show how the ledger computes. κ = 0.5 is a declared policy value, so the result demonstrates arithmetic, not a measured outcome. A member stewards an emergency-ready tile for 40 hours, rated SOUND (q = 0.75). Their resonance reading drifted from D = 0.10 to D = 0.35 during the episode, so ΔD = 0.25.

1. Merit: M rises by q × Q = 0.75 × 40 = 30 merit units.
2. Restoration: V rises by f(0.25, 40). With the proposed starting calibration in §6, that is 0.25 × 40 × 0.5 = 5 hours of restoration owed.
3. The member was never RED, so no NECESSITY override fired and merit was earned normally.
4. A second claim on the same episode is rejected by episode\_id.

**Invariants.** The floor is never scored, decremented or conditioned. A failed Liability is a correction, not a compounding debt. The Quality and Quantity weighting is proposed and must be ratified by an MIEVM round before use.

## 6. Measurement and restoration math

Restoration owed is now computed in restoration units, through a conversion that must be calibrated against real recovery data. v1 added a unitless index to a floor of goods; v3 does not.

### Restoration and merit

```latex
V_i = N_i + \sum_k \kappa \cdot \Delta D_k \cdot \tau_k \qquad M_i = \sum_k q_k \cdot Q_k
```

- V: restoration owed to person i, in restoration-hours (rest, care, shelter or travel valued in hours of recovery).
- N: the unconditional floor, in the same units, set by the User-GROUP SLA.
- ΔD: resonance deficit an episode left behind (unitless, 0 to 1). τ: episode duration in hours.
- κ: conversion rate, restoration-hours per deficit-hour. Proposed starting value 0.5, to be fitted against independently measured recovery time in the F1 study. Until fitted, κ is a declared policy parameter, not a law.
- V and M are never multiplied together, so straining harder never earns more merit.

**Settlement rules.** A deficit is repaid once (episode\_id bar). V does not compound. It settles when the person's reading returns to GREEN or the owed hours are delivered, whichever comes first. A per-period cap prevents runaway claims; the floor is outside the cap.

### The resonance index, specified

| Element | v3 specification (proposed, to be pre-registered) |
| --- | --- |
| Coherence C(t) | Composite of three personal z-scores: heart-rate variability (RMSSD), sleep regularity, and a validated wellbeing self-report (WHO-5). Mapped to (0, 1\] by a logistic, so C is bounded and C₀ > 0 by construction |
| Baseline C₀ | Median C over the person's first 28 consented days; refreshed every 90 days; never set against a population norm |
| Drift | D(t) = \[C₀ − C(t)\] / C₀, clipped to 0 to 1 |
| Bands | GREEN D < 0.15 · YELLOW 0.15 to 0.35 · RED D > 0.35 or a self-reported crisis (thresholds proposed; fixed at pre-registration) |
| Missing data | No reading, no ΔD. Restoration remains available through the floor and responder judgment |
| Sensor disagreement | Median of available channels; disagreement above a set margin is flagged and the reading is not used for allocation |
| Stress vs illness | Illness reports route to the Health pillar; they never reduce merit or restoration |

**Critical safeguard.** RED triggers supportive review and the NECESSITY override: restoration delivered now. A reading can never restrict rights, reduce merit or trigger any consequential decision against the person.

**Data posture.** Readings are sense-to-actuate: computed on the person's device, used in the loop, retained at or near zero, owned by the person under IDIPITIS biometric sovereignty. Only the derived ΔD and a hash reach the ledger. Validation data for F1 is consented separately and firewalled from operations.

**Status.** Specified, not validated. Whether D(t) tracks real strain is F1. Until F1 passes, the mapping is ANALOGICAL and κ is policy.

## 7. Provisioning: entitlement vs verified delivery

An unconditional entitlement is a promise; a verified delivery is a fact. v3 keeps the two apart and makes every floor obligation name a provider, a reserve and a fallback.

### The number anyone can call for REAL HELP

Ground Level is the National Bio-Ecologic Resilience System (NBERS) line. A caller is always admitted; anything that must be checked is held under investigation, never turned away. Admission is by present condition, not history: reentry, hospital discharge, disaster displacement, flight from violence, foster exit and military separation are inflows, not definitions.

The Enneagram's nine positions shape how help is offered (pace, framing, which rung first), never whether. v1's claim that 7 seats × 9 positions gives every caller a seat "by construction" is withdrawn as a guarantee: it describes coverage of the intake design, not representation.

### Provisioning record for each floor obligation

| Element | Required |

A dead channel voids the SLA. An undelivered call is logged as a failure, not a deferral, and counts against F3.

### MIEVM protocol (v3)

1. **Independence.** At least five nodes; no author-side node counts; at least two must be human reviewers or institutions, not AI models.
2. **Blind protocol.** Nodes receive the specification and rubric, not prior scores.

4. **Dispersion.** Report mean, spread and dissent. A spread above 2.0 points blocks SOUND until the divergence is explained.
5. **Conflicts.** Each node declares conflicts; a declared conflict excludes the node.
6. **Record.** Scores, rubric and transcripts published.
